Play-by-play

    The playbook key

    What the traffic means on the field

    Every network event has a football twin. The mapping is a metaphor, so a hurry-up is a hint to investigate, not a verdict.

    Under the hood

    Honest, local, readable

    Parsed in your tab

    A small JavaScript reader handles classic pcap and pcapng (Ethernet, raw IP, Linux cooked). No upload, no server, no analytics.

    Flows, not guesses

    Packets are stitched into TCP flows with handshake, retransmit and reset tracking, plus DNS, UDP and ICMP groups.

    Heuristics, labelled

    Scans, beacons and DNS tunnelling use simple, explainable rules. Treat the coach's call as a lead to check, not a ruling.

    Yardage is bytes

    Yards use a log scale of bytes moved, so a 40 KB fetch and a 400 MB haul both fit on a 100 yard field.
